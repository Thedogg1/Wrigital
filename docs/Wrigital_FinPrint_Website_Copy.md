# Client Intelligence Engine

*URL: /client-intelligence-engine*

**Hero**

*H1:* The Client Intelligence Engine.

*Subhead:* A glass-box AI system for regulated advice. Every figure auditable, every calculation transparent, and a licensed adviser in control of every output.

*Buttons:* [See FinPrint] [Book an assessment]

**Most AI is a black box. Regulated advice cannot use one.** *(H2)*

A general model produces an answer you cannot check. For a supervised profession, that is the problem, not the product. An adviser who cannot show how a number was reached cannot stand behind it, and a compliance officer cannot sign off what they cannot see.

We built the engine to remove that objection.

**Glass-box, by design** *(H2)*

The engine rests on four commitments:

*Deterministic calculation.* Figures come from fixed formulas, not a model's best guess, so the same inputs return the same outputs every time.

*Source attribution.* Every claim traces to where it came from.

*The number audit.* Every figure is checked against its formula before it reaches a report.

*The audit trail.* Every step is recorded, so the whole chain survives inspection after the fact.

**The system proposes. The adviser disposes.** *(H2)*

For each client, the engine drafts a blueprint. It selects the niche frameworks, report sections and formulas that fit, and writes them into a payload the adviser opens. The adviser inspects it, edits it and owns every choice in it.

The AI drafts from a library we built and you can inspect. The adviser decides. So the reasons for what a client sees sit with the licensed professional, not the machine.

**Depth comes from focus** *(H2)*

Building a niche produces a governed library: The frameworks, report sections, formulas and knowledge base for one world, held in one place. For each client, the engine selects from that library, and the adviser confirms the selection.

A narrow niche gives a deep library, and a deep library gives a sharper report. That is why we build for one niche at a time.

**The engine supports. The adviser determines.** *(H2)*

The engine analyses, drafts and documents. It does not make suitability determinations, give advice or approve a strategy. Those belong to the licensed professional, who reviews the analysis, applies judgment and takes responsibility for the outcome.

The engine exists to make that judgment faster to reach and easier to evidence.

**Built to a banking standard** *(H2)*

Our roots are in banking-grade compliance systems, where AI that cannot be explained has never been an option. We built the engine to that standard, with multiple verification touchpoints, deterministic execution and no silent failures.

This is compliance-first architecture, from people who built compliance systems inside a bank.

**See it applied, or put it to work** *(H2)*

FinPrint is the engine applied to one niche. The consultancy brings the same engine, and the same discipline, to your firm.
